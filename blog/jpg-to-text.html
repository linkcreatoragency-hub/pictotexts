JPG to Text: Convert JPG/JPEG Images to Editable Text Free

2026-08-08 · 6 min read

JPG is the default format of nearly every phone camera and digital camera on the planet, which makes it the format people most often need to convert to text. A photo of a contract, a receipt, a whiteboard, a book page, a business card — they're all JPGs. This guide shows you how to convert JPG to text accurately, and how to fix the photo problems that trip OCR up.

  2. Drag your JPG onto the upload box (or click it and choose the file). You can add up to 10 JPGs at once.
  3. Select the language of the text, wait a few seconds, then copy or download the extracted text.

Why JPG Photos Are Harder for OCR Than Screenshots

Unlike a screenshot, a JPG photo passes through a camera — and cameras introduce problems that OCR engines have to fight:

  • Compression artifacts. JPG is a lossy format. At high compression, letter edges get fuzzy "halos" that can turn an e into a c or an o.
  • Perspective distortion. Photographing a page at an angle makes lines converge, which breaks line detection.
  • Uneven lighting. Shadows across the page create dark regions where characters disappear.
  • Focus blur. A slightly missed focus is invisible at thumbnail size but fatal for small print.
  • Page curvature. Open books curve near the spine, warping the text lines.

The good news: all of these are avoidable at capture time.

How to Photograph a Document for Perfect OCR

  • Shoot from directly above. Hold the phone parallel to the page, not at an angle. Use both hands or rest your elbows on the table.
  • Fill the frame. The more pixels per letter, the better. Get close enough that the page occupies most of the photo.
  • Use daylight or two light sources. One lamp from the side creates shadows; light from two sides (or diffuse daylight) keeps the page even.
  • Flatten the page. Press book pages flat, or copy the two halves in separate photos.
  • Tap to focus on the text before shooting, and take two or three shots — one will be sharper than the rest.
  • Skip the digital zoom. Move closer instead; digital zoom just enlarges blur.

What Can You Convert from JPG?

Anything with printed text: receipts and invoices for expense tracking, contracts and letters for digital archiving, book and magazine pages for quoting and study notes, business cards for contact entry, product labels and ingredient lists, posters, signs and menus — including foreign-language ones you want to translate. For handwritten JPGs, see our dedicated handwriting to text guide.

JPG vs PNG for Text Extraction

If you have a choice, PNG beats JPG for OCR because it compresses without losing detail — letter edges stay crisp. In practice the difference only matters for small print: a well-lit, sharp JPG of normal-size text converts at essentially the same accuracy as PNG. Rule of thumb: screenshots → PNG, camera photos → JPG at the highest quality setting your phone offers.
