Screenshot to Text: Copy Text from Any Screenshot (2026 Guide)

2026-08-08 · 7 min read

Screenshots are everywhere: error messages you need to Google, quotes from videos, paragraphs from apps that block copying, code snippets from tutorials, addresses from chat conversations. The problem is always the same — the text is trapped inside the image. Here is every practical way to convert a screenshot to text, from fastest to most advanced.

The Fastest Method: Paste Your Screenshot into an OCR Tool

The quickest workflow doesn't even require saving a file:

  1. Take a screenshot (see the shortcuts for your device below).
  2. Open our free screenshot to text converter.
  3. Press Ctrl+V (Windows) or Cmd+V (Mac) anywhere on the page — the screenshot in your clipboard is picked up automatically.
  4. Wait a few seconds and copy your extracted text.

Because the OCR runs inside your browser, the screenshot never leaves your computer — useful when it contains private conversations, account details or work data.

Screenshot Shortcuts for Every Device

Windows

Press Win+Shift+S to open the Snipping Tool overlay and drag over the area you need. The capture goes straight to your clipboard — perfect for the paste method above. PrtScn captures the whole screen, and Alt+PrtScn captures the active window.

Mac

Press Cmd+Shift+4 to capture a selected area (add Ctrl to send it to the clipboard instead of the desktop), or Cmd+Shift+3 for the full screen.

Android

Press Power+Volume Down. Then open the screenshot in your browser on our converter, or share it to your computer first.

iPhone

Press Side Button+Volume Up. iPhones also offer Live Text: touch and hold text inside a screenshot to select it. When Live Text struggles — small fonts, low contrast, unusual layouts — a dedicated OCR tool usually does better.

Why Copy Text from a Screenshot Instead of Retyping?

  • Error messages: paste the exact error into a search engine or a support ticket without typos changing the meaning.
  • Code snippets: tutorials in video form often show code you can't select — screenshot it and extract clean, copyable code.
  • Protected content: some PDFs, apps and websites disable text selection. A screenshot plus OCR gets you the text for legitimate personal use, like quoting or note-taking.
  • Chat and social apps: lift addresses, phone numbers and instructions out of WhatsApp, Messenger or Instagram screenshots.
  • Meeting slides: capture a slide during a video call and convert it to notes afterwards.

Getting the Best Accuracy from Screenshot OCR

Screenshots are usually the easiest content for OCR because the text is digitally rendered — no camera blur, no shadows, no page curvature. A few tips still help:

  • Capture at 100% zoom or higher. Tiny text in a zoomed-out window gives the engine fewer pixels to work with. Zoom the page in before capturing if the font is small.
  • Crop to the text you need. Icons, buttons and interface chrome around the text can be misread as characters.
  • Prefer dark text on light backgrounds. If you use dark mode, results are still good, but high-contrast light mode is ideal.
  • Watch out for columns. If the screenshot contains side-by-side columns, crop and convert them one at a time to keep the reading order clean.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I convert a screenshot to text without installing anything?

Yes. Browser-based OCR tools like PicToTexts need no installation, no extension and no account. If you can take a screenshot, you can convert it.

Does screenshot OCR work with dark mode?

Yes — modern OCR engines handle light text on dark backgrounds well. If accuracy dips, invert the colors or re-capture in light mode.

Is it safe for screenshots with private information?

With server-based tools, your screenshot is uploaded and may be retained. PicToTexts processes images entirely in your browser, so private screenshots stay on your device. Learn more in our guide to how OCR works.
