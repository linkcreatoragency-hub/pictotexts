About PicToTexts

The story behind the free picture to text converter.

PicToTexts.com was created with one simple goal: to make converting a picture to text fast, free and private for everyone.

We noticed that most OCR tools online either hide their best features behind paywalls, force you to create an account, limit how many images you can convert, or quietly upload your files to their servers. We believed there was a better way.

What Makes Us Different

PicToTexts runs entirely in your browser. When you upload a picture, the OCR engine processes it on your own device — your images are never sent to us or anyone else. That means the sensitive documents, ID cards, private notes and screenshots you convert stay completely private.

The tool is also genuinely free. There are no daily limits, no premium tiers, no watermarks and no sign-up requirements. Whether you convert one screenshot a month or a hundred scanned pages a day, the experience is the same.

Our Technology

PicToTexts is powered by Tesseract, one of the world's most respected open-source OCR engines, originally developed at HP Labs and later sponsored by Google. It supports more than 100 languages and delivers excellent accuracy on printed text. We package this engine to run directly in your web browser using modern WebAssembly technology.

Who We Serve

Every day, students use PicToTexts to digitize lecture slides, professionals extract text from scanned contracts and receipts, translators pull foreign text from photos, and everyday users copy text from screenshots that can't otherwise be selected. If you have text trapped inside an image, we help you set it free.
