Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 7, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how PicToTexts.com ("we", "us", or "our") handles information when you use our website and picture to text conversion tool.

1. Your Images Never Leave Your Device

The core promise of PicToTexts: images you convert are processed entirely within your web browser. They are never uploaded to, stored on, or transmitted through our servers. Once you close or refresh the page, the images and extracted text exist only where you saved them.

2. Information We Collect

Because conversion happens in your browser, we do not collect the content of your images or the text extracted from them. Like most websites, we may collect limited technical information automatically, including browser type, device type, operating system, pages visited, approximate location (country/city level) and referral source. This data is aggregated and cannot be used to identify you personally.

3. Cookies and Advertising

We may use cookies and similar technologies to operate the site, remember preferences and analyze traffic. We use Google AdSense to display advertisements. Google, as a third-party vendor, uses cookies (including the DoubleClick cookie) to serve ads based on your prior visits to this and other websites. You may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings. Third-party vendors and ad networks may also serve ads on our site; you can opt out of some third-party cookies at www.aboutads.info.

4. Analytics

We may use privacy-respecting analytics tools to understand how visitors use the site (for example, which pages are most popular). This information is aggregated and anonymized.

5. Third-Party Links

Our website and blog may contain links to external sites. We are not responsible for the privacy practices or content of those sites and encourage you to read their privacy policies.

6. Children's Privacy

Our service is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

7. Data Security

Since your images are processed locally and we do not operate user accounts, the amount of data we handle is minimal by design. Our website is served over HTTPS encryption.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated revision date.

9. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, contact us at support@pictotexts.com or through our contact page.
